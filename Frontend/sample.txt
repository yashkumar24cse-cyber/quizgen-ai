The solar system consists of the Sun and the objects that orbit it. These include the eight planets: Mercury, Venus, Earth, Mars, Jupiter, Saturn, Uranus, and Neptune. The Sun is a star at the center of the solar system, containing 99.8% of all the system's mass. Earth is the third planet from the Sun and the only astronomical object known to harbor life. Gravity is the force that keeps the planets in orbit around the Sun and moons in orbit around their respective planets. Mars is often called the Red Planet because of the iron oxide prevalent on its surface, which gives it a reddish appearance. Jupiter is the largest planet in the solar system, known for its Great Red Spot, a giant storm. Saturn is famous for its extensive ring system, which is composed of ice particles, rocky debris, and dust.
